About AI Career Connect

AI Career Connect is a full-stack Flask application that combines the power of Mistral AI with modern web technologies to deliver personalised career guidance. Whether you're a fresh graduate or an experienced professional looking for a career pivot, our platform analyses your skills, interests, and experience to recommend actionable career paths.

Key Technologies

  • Flask — lightweight Python web framework
  • SQLite — zero-config relational database
  • Mistral AI — state-of-the-art LLM for career advice
  • SpeechRecognition + gTTS — voice I/O
  • Chart.js — dynamic dashboard visualisations
